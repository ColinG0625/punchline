Colin's weekly mileage across six Illinois stores, much of it to look at a punch item, show it to a sub, or let the owner see it fixed. With the pin and the photo in one place, those trips go away.

0site visits Owner sign-off without a trip

The owner reviews each before and after pair from his desk and accepts or sends it back with a note. Substantial and final completion are signed in the app and the sign-off form prints from the live record.

Owner role in Punchline
1day Proof the same day the work is done

A sub's phone photo is the completion. Verification and owner acceptance can follow the same day, so the item is closed and the sub's final payment is not waiting on someone driving out to look.

Photo required to mark complete
1list A frozen list, agreed by the owner

One or two new items were being added every visit. Once the owner accepts the store list it freezes. Anything added later needs an unlock, recorded with who opened it and when, so the list the subs are chased on is the list the owner agreed to.

Store review, 10/27/2026: Nick Brimie on list creep
1record each A record of record in JobPlanner

Every item exports as one record to the store's JobPlanner punch list with its location, photos and who closed it. Projects started in JobPlanner finish in JobPlanner; Punchline feeds it instead of replacing it.
